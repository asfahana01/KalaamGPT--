01.

THE MAN BEHIND THE VISION

From selling newspapers in Rameswaram to leading India's scientific and presidential office.

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam

15 October 1931 – 27 July 2015

A Life Dedicated to Knowledge & Nation Building

Born into a humble Tamil Muslim family in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Dr. Kalam learned perseverance early in life. To support his family, he distributed newspapers as a young boy while remaining fiercely dedicated to his studies.

He graduated in Aerospace Engineering from the prestigious Madras Institute of Technology in 1960. Joining DRDO and subsequently ISRO, he served as the Project Director of India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-3), successfully deploying the Rohini satellite in 1980.

As Director of DRDO, Dr. Kalam spearheaded the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP), conceptualizing and developing strategic missiles including Agni and Prithvi, earning him the title of "Missile Man of India".

In 2002, he was elected as the 11th President of India, celebrated affectionately across the nation as "The People's President". After his presidency, he devoted every day to teaching youth at IIMs, IITs, and universities across India until his final moments in 2015.

CHRONICLES OF ILLUSTRIOUS JOURNEY

Milestones that transformed Indian science, space, defence, and governance.

1931

Born in Rameswaram

Born on October 15 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Raised with strong values of humility, discipline, and interfaith harmony.

1960

Aerospace Engineering & DRDO

Graduated from Madras Institute of Technology in Aerospace Engineering and joined DRDO as a senior scientific assistant.

1969

ISRO & SLV-3 Success

Transferred to ISRO as Project Director of SLV-3, successfully putting the Rohini satellite into orbit in July 1980.

1982

Missile Development Programme

Appointed Director of DRDO. Spearheaded the creation of Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Trishul, and Nag missiles.

1998

Pokhran-II & Scientific Advisor

Chief Scientific Advisor to the PM and Director General of DRDO during the successful Pokhran-II nuclear tests.

2002

11th President of India

Elected 11th President of India with overwhelming cross-party support. Became known as "The People's President".

2015

Eternal Inspiration

Passed away peacefully on July 27 while delivering a lecture to students at IIM Shillong, leaving an unperishable legacy.
